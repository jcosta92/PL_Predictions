How it works

Elo ratings updated after every match (bigger wins move the rating more, promoted sides start below the league mean), recent form over the last 5 and 10 matches, rest days, and a multinomial logistic regression on top. Everything was tested against the bookmakers with paired statistics, including head-to-head history, weather, fixture congestion, manager changes, expected goals, player ratings, suspensions and confirmed lineups. Only lineups moved the number. Code, data and the full write-up: github.com/jcosta92/PL_Predictions.
